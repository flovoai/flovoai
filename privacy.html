What we collect

When you check your Flow Score

When you complete the Flow Score on our website, we collect:

  • Your contact details: your name, email address and, if you give it, your company name.
  • About your business: your industry, type of business, team size and, if you choose to give it, your turnover.
  • Your answers: how your business currently works, including the hours spent on admin and the hourly cost you enter.
  • Your results: your Flow Score, area scores and the cost figures worked out from your answers.

The questions are about your business, not about you personally. Please don't include sensitive personal information in your answers.

When you book a call

Calls are booked through Cal.com. When you book, Cal.com collects the details you enter, such as your name, email and any notes, and shares them with us so we can hold the call. If you've just completed the Flow Score, the booking form is filled in with your name, email and a short summary of your results so we can come prepared. You can change or remove any of this before booking.

When you contact us

If you email us, we keep your message and contact details so we can reply.

When you visit the website

We don't use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Like any website, our hosting provider automatically records basic technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, to deliver the site and keep it secure. Our pages also load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google and shares your IP address with them.
